About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering Technology at Farmingdale State College, State University of New York. My academic and professional work spans mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, robotics, automation, artificial intelligence, computer vision, immersive simulation, and engineering education.

My research emphasizes the integration of intelligent computational methods with physical engineering systems. Current interests include collaborative robotics, AI-enabled robotic perception, digital twins, human–computer and human–robot interaction, computer vision, virtual and augmented environments, and innovative approaches to engineering education.

I also have extensive experience in engineering education, curriculum development, applied research, and interdisciplinary engineering projects involving students at both undergraduate and graduate levels.

Research

My research combines artificial intelligence with robotic, cyber-physical, and educational systems, with emphasis on applied engineering problems and human-centered technology.

Robotics & Intelligent Automation

Collaborative robotics, robotic perception, intelligent automation, motion control, autonomous systems, and human–robot interaction.

Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision

Deep learning, object detection, pose estimation, multimodal sensing, generative AI, and AI-enabled engineering applications.

Digital Twins & Cyber-Physical Systems

Physical–virtual synchronization, real-time motion mapping, simulation, virtual robotic environments, and data-driven cyber-physical systems.

Engineering Education

AI-supported learning, flipped classrooms, collaborative learning, virtual laboratories, open educational resources, and emerging educational technologies.

Virtual & Mixed Reality

Immersive engineering environments, Unity and Unreal Engine applications, virtual laboratories, simulation, and human–computer interaction.

Mechatronics & Smart Systems

Sensors, actuators, embedded systems, industrial automation, control, IoT, and integrated electromechanical systems.
