About

I am an engineering researcher and educator working at the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, computer vision, digital twins, human–computer interaction, and engineering education.

My work combines computational methods with physical engineering systems, with particular interests in intelligent automation, collaborative robotics, AI-enabled engineering tools, and immersive simulation.
